        InitStream[ix: initialize_stream]

            SignerCheck{Verify Payer / Recipient Signer}

    %% Interactions
    Payer -->|1. Init & Deposit Tokens| InitStream
    InitStream -->|Derives & Stores| StreamState
    InitStream -->|Transfers Principal via CPI| SPLToken
    SPLToken -->|Holds Funds| EscrowVault

    Keeper -->|2. Route Idle Liquidity| DepositYield
    DepositYield -->|CPI: Deposit Unclaimed Principal| YieldVault

    Recipient -->|3. Claim Vested Liquidity| ClaimStream
    ClaimStream --> SignerCheck
    SignerCheck --> TimeCheck
    TimeCheck --> BalanceCheck
    BalanceCheck --> StateCheck
    StateCheck -->|CPI: Transfer Claimable to Recipient| SPLToken
    SPLToken -->|Receive Vested Payout| Recipient

    Payer -->|4. Clawback Unvested Balance| CancelStream
    CancelStream --> SignerCheck
    CancelStream -->|CPI: Unvested -> Payer, Vested -> Recipient| SPLToken
